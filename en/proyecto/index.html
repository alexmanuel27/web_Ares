The Project

A laboratory of its own for the bay

CubanWaterLab is the working name of the project “In situ monitoring of water quality in Cuban bays: creating and promoting the use of scientific tools,” a five-year cooperation between the University of Havana and the Université Libre de Bruxelles.

To provide the Cuban scientific-social ecosystem with a set of tools that enables continuous, in situ monitoring of water-quality parameters in Cuban bays, in a sustainable way and connected to local communities.

  • Design and build a wave channel at the Faculty of Physics of the University of Havana
  • Design and build instrumented buoys and low-cost portable tools for in situ measurement
  • Set up a server with a website to receive data from the buoys and from the community
  • Establish a methodology for using and maintaining the buoys together with fishers and communities
  • Train the corresponding human resources, in Cuba and in Belgium

Dr. C. Ernesto Altshuler Álvarez — Project director, April–June 2026 report

Funding

CubanWaterLab exists thanks to the financial support of ARES (Académie de Recherche et d'Enseignement Supérieur), the Belgian federation of higher-education institutions that drives university development-cooperation projects. ARES funds the project; it is neither its name nor its author.

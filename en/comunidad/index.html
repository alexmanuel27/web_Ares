Voices from the bay

CubanWaterLab's social-science component works alongside the Casablanca community — fishers, students, local authorities — so that the technology answers real needs, not just laboratory questions.

“This is the first time a project has been carried out that directly involves them.”

Fishers from the Casablanca recreational fishing base, April–June 2025 meeting

“They consider it important to have a new technology that lets them learn about the bay's water parameters… regarding pollution, they raise the need to know the degree of oil contamination.”

Quarterly report, April–June 2025

“They suggest placing the buoys near a spot where they can keep an eye on them.”

Views gathered at the first meeting with fishers

Building on Casablanca's environmental and cultural diagnosis (2024) and on 87 of its own surveys, the social-science team works with two focus groups — accredited fishers and secondary-school students — and collaborates with the community's Popular Council to design Green Map workshops and citizen-science actions.
